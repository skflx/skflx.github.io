Hearing research study

Are you considering or scheduled for a second cochlear implant?

Volunteers needed for a study on how the brain hears differently between a cochlear implant and a hearing aid. We want to understand how the brain combines sounds from a cochlear implant and a hearing aid, and how that changes after a second implant. Taking part helps researchers better understand hearing for future patients.

Who can join

  • Adults 18 and older
  • You use a cochlear implant on one side and a hearing aid on the other
  • You are scheduled for, a candidate for, or being evaluated for a second cochlear implant at OHSU
  • You are comfortable listening and speaking in English

What's involved

  • Two study sessions of 4-6 hours each (can be split into shorter sessions)
    • Session 1: Before your second implant
    • Session 2: About 3-6 months after the second implant
  • Listening to sounds and pressing a button on a touchscreen to indicate what you heard

You will receive $25 per hour for your time, plus compensation for travel costs. Scheduling is flexible and can be coordinated with clinic follow-up visits.

Taking part is completely voluntary. Your decision will not affect the care you receive at OHSU.
