# Attribution: CT images

Every file below is a slice of the *Interactive Web-based CT Temporal Bone Module*, University of Washington Department of Radiology (a right temporal bone, unlabelled slice cropped to the bone and recompressed). Credit is required wherever the image is shown.

The crops and the tappable regions drawn on them are generated by `tools/tbone-pipeline/slices.py` from the UW atlas's own slices and label tips (`tbone/geometry/landmarks.json`); rerun it, never hand-edit a crop or a region. A region is a fixed-radius pointer at a UW label tip, not a segmentation, and is draft until the owner vets it.
