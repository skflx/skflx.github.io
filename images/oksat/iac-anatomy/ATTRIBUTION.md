# Attribution: raster images

Every UW CT file below is a slice of the *Interactive Web-based CT Temporal Bone Module*, University of Washington Department of Radiology (a right temporal bone, unlabelled slice cropped to the bone and recompressed). Every Stanford figure below is an unmodified JPEG from the *Stanford Skull Base Surgery Atlas* (Jackler RK, Gralapp C; Thieme 2009 / Stanford Medicine). Credit is required wherever an image is shown.

The UW crops and the tappable regions drawn on them are generated by `tools/tbone-pipeline/slices.py --guide iac-anatomy` from the UW atlas's own slices and label tips (`tbone/geometry/landmarks.json`); rerun it, never hand-edit a crop or a region. A region is a fixed-radius pointer at a UW label tip, not a segmentation, and is draft until the owner vets it.
