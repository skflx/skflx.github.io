## How to read a note

- Notes tagged `#unvetted` are study scaffolding, not yet checked against source texts. They carry a warning at the top. Nothing here is a clinical reference.
- Every page ends with **Suggest a correction**: it opens a public GitHub issue naming the note. Corrections are made in the source vault and republished, and the issue closes when the fix goes live.
- Tiers run from **I** (bedrock foundations) to **VII** (integration: emergencies, complications, free flaps).
