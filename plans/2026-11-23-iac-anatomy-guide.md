## 0. Context

A ground-up OKSAT Interactive learning guide on the **internal auditory canal (IAC)**, plus its
companion question module, for OHNS residents PGY-1 to PGY-5 (OTE, ABOHNS written, neurotology OR
readiness). The base is the owner's educational blueprint (prepared with Neon), reproduced in substance in
§5 with the orchestrator's additions marked **[+]**.

**Thesis.** Residents rarely miss IAC questions for want of "Seven-Up, Coke-Down". They miss them
because the IAC is a ~10 mm tube whose contents rotate between brainstem and fundus, and whose
surgical anatomy flips with the vector of entry: lateral (translabyrinthine), superior (middle
cranial fossa, MCF) or medial (retrosigmoid). The guide builds the bony box, then the wiring, then
reads it on imaging, then enters it in the OR, then explains disease by mechanism.

Model: `ar-crs-aerd` (mechanism-first, retrieval prompts in every section, every section drilling into
modules) with the anatomy blocks built for `ear-anatomy` (`hotspot`, `layers`, `neighbors`, `sequence`).

**Scope boundaries.**
- Defer to `ear-anatomy`: external and middle ear, mastoidectomy steps, tympanic and mastoid facial
  nerve, broad petrous apex pneumatization. Name them as neighbouring walls; do not re-teach.
- Defer to `vestibular-schwannoma` and `temporal-bone-trauma`: management algorithms (observation vs
  SRS vs microsurgery), full trauma work-up. Pathology here stays anchored to anatomical mechanism:
  compression, bottleneck ischaemia, corridors, hazards.

### 4.4 Section `ref` (I1.1, I1.2)

- **Data.** A section may carry `ref: ['Otology/<note title>', …]`: the note's path under the wiki's
  `content/` without `.md`, exactly as the file is named.
- **Snapshot.** `tools/skoto-notes.mjs` lists the wiki's notes (from a local clone of
  `skflx/ohns-wiki-skflx` or `gh api …/git/trees/HEAD?recursive=1`) and writes
  `tools/skoto-notes.json` (`{ generated, notes: [path…] }`). It lives in `tools/` and is never served.
- **Check.** `check-data` fails a `ref` not in the snapshot, a `ref` on a chapter (not a section), and
  a duplicate within a section.
- **Render.** When `OKSAT_SKOTO.live` is true, the chapter foot shows *Related in sk.oto*: one link per
  distinct note cited in the chapter, in first-cited order, each followed by the section numbers that
  cite it. Href = `OKSAT_SKOTO.base` + the Quartz slug of the path. The executor derives the slug rule
  from Quartz's `slugifyFilePath` in the wiki repo and proves it on three live notes (HTTP 200).
  Text via `textContent`; links `rel="noopener"`; nothing rendered when `live` is false or no
  section cites a note.

## 5. Chapters (outline; prose waits for the ledger)

> **Superseded in detail by the chapter briefs** ([`plans/iac-briefs/`](iac-briefs/README.md)), which pin
> every claim per section. Where this outline and a brief differ, the brief wins.

`[G]` = a `[GROUND]` value, written only from the ledger. **[+]** = orchestrator addition to the
owner blueprint. Misconceptions (§5.9) become `call k:'trap'` blocks in the named section. Section ids
double as module concept keys.

**0 · `start`** — Thesis; learning map (Bony box → Rotating wiring → Reading the tube → Entering the
tube → Mechanisms); tier targets (Junior: fundal quadrants, crests, dimensions, normal 4-dot MRI,
basic tumour localization. Senior: six-wall neighbourhood, rotation, MCF landmark geometry, approach
selection by audiogram and fundal extent. Chief: meatal foramen bottleneck, CND / BCNC / IP-III CI
hazards, retrosigmoid lateral blind spot, AICA / subarcuate salvage, CSF leak tracts).

**1 · `osteology` — Bony architecture and the six-wall neighbourhood** (35 min)
- `os-orient` Petrous pyramid ~45° anteromedial vs IAC axis near-coronal, roughly collinear with the
  EAC [G]. Figure F8.
- `os-dev` **[+]** Embryology: the otic capsule ossifies around the VIII nerve and its ganglion, so IAC
  calibre tracks nerve presence; sets up CND and stenosis in ch. 4 [G].
- `os-zones` Porus (oval; sharp posterior lip vs bevelled anterior), canal proper, fundus (lamina
  cribrosa abutting the otic capsule).
- `os-morph` Length, vertical diameter, asymmetry, flaring and stenosis cutoffs [G].
- `os-walls` Six walls and what lies beyond each (F1, `neighbors`): superior (MCF floor, arcuate
  eminence / SSCC, GSPN, geniculate, superior petrosal sinus medially); inferior (jugular bulb,
  cochlear aqueduct as the inferior limit above IX–XI); anterior (basal turn, petrous ICA, apex);
  posterior (posterior fossa dura, vestibular aqueduct / operculum, PSCC and common crus); lateral
  (vestibule, cochlea); medial (CPA, brainstem).
- `os-cells` Perilabyrinthine tracts (supra-, infralabyrinthine, retrofacial) around the IAC; sets
  up CSF rhinorrhea after a sealed dural closure (resolved in `cc-csf`).

**2 · `fundus` — Fundus, crests and the meatal bottleneck** (35 min)
- `fu-crests` Transverse (falciform) crest; vertical crest (Bill's bar) in the superior compartment
  only.
- `fu-quadrants` Anterosuperior VII → labyrinthine segment; posterosuperior SVN → utricle, SSCC, LSCC
  (+ part of saccule via Voit's nerve [G]); anteroinferior cochlear → tractus spiralis foraminosus →
  modiolus; posteroinferior IVN → saccule. Figure F2 (`hotspot`, frames per ear and per view).
- `fu-singular` Foramen singulare and the singular canal (posterior ampullary nerve); **[+]** its
  relation to the round window niche as the singular neurectomy landmark [G].
- `fu-cnc` **[+]** Bony cochlear nerve canal as anatomy (measured in `im-cnd`) [G].
- `fu-meatal` Meatal foramen: narrowest point of the fallopian canal [G]; arachnoid sleeve ends,
  epineurium thickens; why edema here blocks conduction (to `cc-bell`).
- `fu-views` Rotating the fundus: the same four nerves seen on oblique-sagittal MRI, translab, MCF and
  retrosigmoid, right and left (F2 frames).

**3 · `neurovascular` — Contents, rotation and meningeal planes** (45 min)
- `nv-cpa` Root exit at the pontomedullary junction: VII anterior to VIII [G].
- `nv-rotation` Brainstem → porus → mid-canal → fundus rotation and the split of VIII into three
  divisions. **Source conflict:** the owner blueprint says ~90°; the Stanford atlas (Jackler, fig. 6)
  says "gradual 180 degree rotation" and that mid-canal positions are far more variable than at the
  fundus. Degree and handedness per ear are decided in the ledger and taught as a `trap` labelled
  "Source conflict". Figure F3 (`sequence` of four cuts).
- `nv-intermedius` Course between VII and VIII, adherent to VIII in the CPA, joins VII in the canal;
  modalities; Hitselberger sign.
- `nv-orz` Obersteiner-Redlich zone: central vs peripheral myelin; segment length VIII vs VII [G];
  why vestibular schwannomas arise in the canal.
- `nv-ganglion` **[+]** Vestibular (Scarpa) ganglion in the lateral canal [G]; why it matters to
  neurectomy and to the cell of origin.
- `nv-anast` (`deep · Chief`) Oort anastomosis, vestibulofacial anastomoses; why sharp dissection.
- `nv-aica` AICA and meatal loops, Chavda types I–III [G]; AICA occlusion = lateral pontine syndrome
  plus total ipsilateral audiovestibular loss. Figure F4.
- `nv-labyrinthine` Labyrinthine (internal auditory) artery: origin [G], end artery, common cochlear
  and anterior vestibular branches; sacrifice = dead ear.
- `nv-subarcuate` Subarcuate artery under the SSCC arch: coagulate and divide, never avulse.
- `nv-meninges` Dura and arachnoid reflections; subarachnoid space to the fundus; the double arachnoid
  plane between tumour capsule and VII / vessels.

**4 · `imaging` — Cross-sectional imaging and congenital variants** (40 min)
- `im-ct-read` HRCT read order: width / symmetry / flaring → crests → labyrinthine VII and geniculate
  → basal turn → jugular bulb height vs IAC floor → vestibular aqueduct / operculum → apex
  pneumatization. UW CT `hotspot` frames.
- `im-mri` Sequences (heavily T2-weighted 3D: CISS / FIESTA / DRIVE; T1 ± gadolinium) and what each
  answers.
- `im-4dot` Oblique-sagittal orientation for right and left; cochlear nerve ≥ VII in area [G];
  vestibular nerves fused medially, separate laterally. Figure F7a.
- `im-fundal-cap` The fundal CSF cap and hearing-preservation feasibility.
- `im-cnd` IAC stenosis, CND (aplasia vs hypoplasia), normal IAC does not exclude CND, BCNC cutoff
  [G]; CI vs ABI candidacy at anatomy level. F7b.
- `im-ip3` IP-III (POU3F4, X-linked): bulbous fundus, absent modiolus, gusher, electrode into the IAC.
  F7c.
- `im-otoscl` **[+]** (`deep · Chief`) Cochlear otosclerosis and IAC-adjacent lucency/diverticula as an
  imaging trap [G].
- `im-lesions` VS (centred on porus, acute angles, porus flaring, ice-cream cone) vs meningioma
  (eccentric, broad base, obtuse angles, dural tail, hyperostosis, calcification) vs facial nerve
  schwannoma (labyrinthine / geniculate extension) vs neuritis (linear fundal VII and geniculate
  enhancement, no mass). **[+]** `deep`: lipoma, epidermoid (DWI), hemangioma. F7d.

**5 · `approaches` — Surgical corridors and perspective shifts** (50 min)
- `ap-frame` Rotate the fundus per vector (F2 views, F6).
- `ap-translab` Hearing sacrificed; 270° skeletonization (superior trough to MCF dura, inferior trough
  to jugular bulb / cochlear aqueduct, posterior wall); Bill's bar between superficial SVN and deep
  VII at the fundus; hazards: high bulb, VII, perilabyrinthine CSF tracts.
- `ap-mcf` Hearing preserved; indication anchor [G]; VII lies superior (anterosuperior, on top of the
  tumour); basal turn anterior, SSCC posterior; temporal lobe retraction, vein of Labbé.
- `ap-mcf-landmarks` House (GSPN → geniculate → labyrinthine VII → IAC), Fisch (blue-line SSCC; IAC
  angle [G]), Garcia-Ibañez (bisect arcuate eminence–GSPN angle [G]). Figure F5.
- `ap-retrosig` Hearing preserved; posterior lip drilling depth [G]; stay medial to the vestibular
  aqueduct / sac, PSCC and common crus; lateral fundus blind spot; VII usually anterior and found late.
- `ap-extend` **[+]** (`deep · Chief`) Retrolabyrinthine, transcochlear / transotic as named
  extensions; vestibular neurectomy (SVN/IVN vs cochlear cleavage plane); ABI through translab to the
  lateral recess.
- `ap-choose` Corridor choice by hearing, tumour size and fundal extent: anatomy only; management
  defers to `vestibular-schwannoma`.

**6 · `clinical-correlations` — Anatomical mechanisms of disease** (40 min)
- `cc-vs-origin` Nerve of origin, IVN vs SVN [G].
- `cc-fn-displacement` VII displacement patterns (anterior / anterosuperior most common; posterior
  rare and dangerous) [G].
- `cc-audiometry` WRS falls out of proportion to PTA: neural dyssynchrony, rollover.
- `cc-grades` Anchor only: Koos I–IV; AAO-HNS A/B, Gardner-Robertson I/II, 50/50 rule [G].
- `cc-hearing-mech` **[+]** Why hearing is lost at surgery: labyrinthine artery spasm or sacrifice,
  cochlear nerve traction, fundal dissection.
- `cc-bell` Meatal foramen edema → ischaemia → Wallerian degeneration; decompression criteria [G];
  why transmastoid decompression misses the lesion.
- `cc-trauma` Otic-capsule-violating fractures through the IAC: immediate profound SNHL plus acute
  VII palsy (labyrinthine / perigeniculate).
- `cc-nvc` AICA loop at the root entry zone: vestibular paroxysmia (VIII), hemifacial spasm (VII).
- `cc-csf` **[+]** CSF leak tracts after IAC surgery: perilabyrinthine cells → antrum / middle ear →
  Eustachian tube → rhinorrhea; why a sealed dura still leaks. Figure F9.

**7 · `synthesis` — Integrated vignettes** (45 min). Each a `check` chain then module cases.
1. Right 1.1 cm intracanalicular VS to the fundus (no CSF cap), PTA 15 dB / WRS 96%: MCF over
   retrosigmoid (blind spot) and translab (hearing); where VII sits on opening the dura from above.
2. 2-year-old, bilateral profound SNHL, normal IAC calibre, BCNC 0.9 mm: CT alone is a trap; the
   absent anteroinferior dot; CI counselling.
3. Right 2.8 cm CPA VS, non-serviceable hearing, high-riding bulb: translab vs retrosigmoid and the
   constraint on the inferior trough.
4. Day-9 Bell's palsy, 93% ENoG degeneration, silent EMG: what to decompress and why MCF.
5. X-linked progressive mixed loss, bulbous fundus: recognize IP-III before stapedotomy or CI.
6. **[+]** Rhinorrhea on day 5 after retrosigmoid VS resection with a watertight dural closure:
   trace the tract (opened posterior-lip air cells → middle ear → ET) and the anatomy of repair.

**8 · `appraisal` — Evidence limits and source appraisal** (20 min). Where sources disagree: IAC
normal / stenotic / dilated cutoffs; IVN vs SVN proportions; degree and handedness of rotation;
MCF size cutoffs (1.0 vs 1.5 vs 2.0 cm); ENoG decompression (Gantz criteria vs non-operative).
**[+]** Aggregation and design notes per claim (cadaver series vs radiologic series vs surgical
case series; measurement plane; selection of referral populations).

### 5.9 Misconceptions (each a `trap` in the named section)

| Misconception | Correction | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Bill's bar divides the whole fundus | Superior compartment only (VII vs SVN) | `fu-crests` |
| The IAC follows the 45° pyramid | Near-coronal, roughly collinear with the EAC | `os-orient` |
| A normal IAC rules out CND | Measure the BCNC; check the anteroinferior dot | `im-cnd` |
| Retrosigmoid reaches the whole canal with hearing | PSCC / common crus block the lateral fundus | `ap-retrosig` |
| In MCF the tumour sits on top of VII | VII is superior, superficial to the tumour | `ap-mcf` |
| VS arises at the transition (Obersteiner-Redlich) zone | A myth (ledger G4): they arise from vestibular Schwann cells within the canal | `nv-orz` |
| **[+]** VII sits at the same clock face along the whole canal | Positions are fixed at the fundus, variable mid-canal | `nv-rotation` |
