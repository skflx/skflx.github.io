beyond each wall. **Ledger:** G5, G8, "Other facts"; HCA3 p42, p45, p49; HCA6 p75–77; K2.

**File:** `iac-walls-box.svg`, viewBox `0 0 760 560`. Right ear.

Title (x 380, y 30, centred): "The internal auditory canal as an opened box (right ear, schematic)".
