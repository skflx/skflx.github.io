# Brief · `appraisal` — Evidence and its limits (ch. 8, ~20 min)

`cards` blocks, one card per disagreement: claim · sources and their numbers · design and why they differ ·
what this guide teaches. Then 1 `check` per section.

## `apr-cutoffs` · IAC size cutoffs
- Card: normal 4–8 mm, stenosis < 2 / < 3 mm, flaring > 8 (> 9) mm, asymmetry > 2 mm [owner; El Sadik 2017;
  Ionescu 2023] vs measured means: adult vertical diameter 4.47 mm in 110 CTs [Marques 2012]; mid-diameter
  4.2 mm in 162 CTs [Gözen 2026, PMID 41682992]. Design: CT series differ in plane and where they measure (mid-canal vs
  porus vs opening width). Taught: the owner's cutoffs, measured perpendicular to the long axis.
- Card: BCNC < 1.4 mm [Stjernholm 2002, casts plus CT] vs other series (Fatterpekar 2000 found smaller canals
  in SNHL without a cutoff in its abstract). Taught: < 1.4 mm.
- **Check:** "Why can two papers disagree on 'normal' IAC width?" → different planes and landmarks.

## `apr-rotation` · How far the nerves turn
- Card: ~90° inside the canal, right CCW (owner; Silverstein 1986; Schefter & Harner 1986) vs "gradual 180°"
  (Stanford/Jackler atlas caption) vs the facial nerve 0° in 37%, 30–90° in 63% (Tian 2008, 30 bones).
  Design: cadaver dissection vs surgical observation; "rotation" of VIII vs of VII. Taught: ~90° for VIII.
- **Check:** "Why might an atlas say 180°?" → different structure or reference frame; 180° is not normal VIII
  anatomy (owner).

## `apr-orz` · The transition zone
- Card: VIII 9–14 mm, VII 2–3 mm (Radiopaedia) vs 3.6 vs 3.7 mm (Gupta 2023, histomorphometry at "maximum
  convexity"). Design: where along the nerve and how the junction is defined. Taught: Radiopaedia distances;
  origin-at-the-zone is a myth.
- **Check:** "Is the transition zone the schwannoma's birthplace?" → no.

## `apr-origin` · Superior or inferior vestibular nerve
- Card: IVN 84.8% (Komatsuzaki 2001, 271 translab cases; intraoperative), "up to 91%" (K31) vs "usually SVN"
  (HCA6). Design: translab series identify the nerve at surgery; large tumours obscure it (5.6%
  undetermined). Taught: IVN commonest.
- **Check:** "What limits nerve-of-origin data?" → large tumours obscure the nerve; surgical series only.

## `apr-mcf-bell` · MCF size and Bell's decompression
- Card: MCF ≤ 2 cm (House Clinic) vs < 1.5 cm (Sanna) — institutional practice, not trials.
- Card: Gantz 1999: 91% vs 58% — prospective but non-randomized, self-selected controls, small groups;
  decompression must be within 14 days. Taught as the criterion, with its design stated.
- Card: MCF landmark angles: Fisch 73.5 ± 11.7°, Garcia-Ibañez 126 ± 12° (bisector 63°) [Sahana 2023] vs
  45–60° (HCA3): wide individual variation (Maina 2007).
- **Check:** "Main weakness of the Gantz series?" → controls self-selected, no randomization.
